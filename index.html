THE RHYTHM OF GANPATI.

Since 2019, Swaranaad Dhol Tasha Hubli has been a traditional Maharashtrian-style Dhol-Tasha Pathak dedicated to bringing the power, discipline and cultural spirit of Dhol-Tasha to Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations in Hubli.

"We don't just play the beat. We become the beat."

Hubli's First

Dhol Tasha Pathak

Born in 2019 from a deep devotion to Ganpati Bappa and a passion for Maharashtrian musical tradition, Swaranaad stands proudly as Hubli's pioneer in authentic Dhol-Tasha performance.

We bring together a community united by discipline, synchronized rhythm, and cultural identity. Every beat of our Dhol and every sharp strike of the Tasha is an offering, preserving a rich heritage of traditional percussion right here in Karnataka.

When we perform, we perform exclusively for Ganesh Chaturthi. It is not just music; it is an emotion, a roar of devotion that transforms the streets into a grand celebration.
